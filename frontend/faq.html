Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about products, payments, delivery, and accounts.

Do you deliver nationwide?

The frontend demonstrates delivery options for customers throughout the Philippines.

What payment methods are available?

GCash, credit card, and cash on delivery are included as simulated options.

Can I request a dental chair quotation?

Yes. Demo equipment listings may use quotation-based ordering in the future backend.

How can I track my order?

Open Order History to view the Pending, Processing, Shipped, and Delivered timeline.

Can dental students create accounts?

Yes. The registration page includes a dental-student account type.

Are payments real?

No. This frontend currently uses a safe payment simulation for academic demonstration.
