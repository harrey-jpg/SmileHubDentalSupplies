🎓Academic demonstration. Delivery estimates, fees, and tracking on this page simulate a checkout flow. No real courier is booked and no parcel moves.

Delivery zones

Metro Manila1–3 business days, Monday to Saturday.
Provincial3–7 business days, depending on the destination.

Timelines are estimates shown before checkout so demo customers know what to expect. A production store would confirm cut-off times and holiday schedules with its courier partners.

Delivery methods

At checkout, demo customers choose between two simulated methods:

  • Standard delivery — 1–3 business days, ₱150 or free over ₱3,000.
  • Express delivery — same or next day where available; shown as a demo option only.

Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is simulated for Metro Manila orders only. Provincial demo orders use GCash or bank transfer at the simulated checkout.

Order tracking

Every demo order appears with a status timeline on the Orders page — from placement through simulated shipment — ready for a future courier integration. Statuses are illustrative and update locally in your browser.

Contact Us

Questions about delivery in this demo? Reach us through the Contact Us page. Support for this academic project is based in Quezon City, Metro Manila.
