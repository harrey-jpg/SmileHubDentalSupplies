About SmileHub

A professional dental supplies marketplace concept for the Philippines.

Our Mission

Make reliable dental supplies easier to find.

SmileHub aims to connect dental clinics, professionals, students, and healthcare teams with a clear and convenient product-shopping experience.

Our Vision

A trusted digital hub for Philippine dentistry.

We envision a marketplace where customers can compare products, understand stock, complete orders, and receive helpful updates in one system.

Project Objectives

Customer Convenience

Simple product discovery, cart, checkout, wishlist, profile, and orders.

Admin Visibility

Dashboard views for products, inventory, customers, reports, and audit logs.

System Integration

A frontend prepared for later connection to Node.js services and MySQL.

Dental deals in your inbox

Receive product updates, clinic essentials, and student-friendly offers.
