Academic Demonstration

This site is a frontend project built for academic purposes. It demonstrates what a dental-supply storefront for Philippine clinics, dentists, and students could look like — but no real orders, shipments, or charges occur.

Product Information

Products, prices, inventory levels, reviews, and delivery details are sample data prepared for demonstration. They do not represent live stock or binding offers, and displayed prices cannot be honored as real quotations.

Payments

GCash (a Philippine mobile-wallet payment method), card, and cash-on-delivery choices are simulations of a checkout flow. No real payment is processed, no gateway is contacted, and no money moves.

Do not enter real payment credentials anywhere on this site.

Equipment Listings

High-value items such as dental chairs and autoclaves (steam sterilizers) are marked as demo or quotation products. In a production store these would require assisted sales, site inspection, and formal written quotations rather than one-click checkout.

Related Policies

Privacy. Your use of this site is subject to our Privacy Policy, which explains demo data handling under the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Shipping. Delivery estimates are frontend demonstrations. See Shipping Information for the simulated workflow.

Returns. Return eligibility is simulated. See our Return Policy for the demonstration workflow.

⚖️Philippine Law

This demonstration is designed to align with Philippine e-commerce and consumer protection standards:

  • RA 8792Electronic Commerce Act — validity of electronic transactions. — Electronic Commerce Act (validity of electronic transactions).
  • RA 7394Consumer Act — consumer rights and fair trade. — Consumer Act of the Philippines (consumer rights and fair trade).
  • RA 10173Data Privacy Act of 2012 — protection of personal information. — Data Privacy Act of 2012 (protection of personal information).

This is a class project, not legal advice. A production store should have these terms reviewed by Philippine counsel and register with the DTI.

Modifications

As an academic project, these terms may change as the frontend evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

Agreement

By accessing this site, you agree to these demonstration terms: you will treat all content as sample data, enter no real personal or payment information, and use the site for evaluation and learning purposes only.

Contact Information

For inquiries, please use the Contact Us page. Support for this academic project is based in Quezon City, Metro Manila.
